# Foreword

```{raw:typst}
#v(20pt)
```

I am proud to introduce the third edition of the North Carolina School of Science and Mathematics’ (NCSSM) scientific journal, *Morganton Scientific*. Each year students at NCSSM conduct significant scientific research and *Morganton Scientific* is a showcase of some of the best research being done by students at NCSSM.

Providing students with opportunities to apply their learning through research is not only vitally important in preparing and exciting students to pursue STEM degrees and careers after high school, but essential to encouraging innovative thinking that allows students to scientifically address major issues and problems we face in the world today and will face in the future. Opened in 1980, NCSSM was the nation's first public residential high school where students study a specialized curriculum emphasizing science, technology, engineering, mathematics, and so much more. With the opening of our Morganton campus in the fall of 2022, we are able to provide more students with opportunities to conduct high-level research in biology, chemistry, physics, the applied sciences and math. Providing these opportunities is critical to NCSSM’s mission to educate academically talented students to become state, national and global leaders and innovators in STEM fields and beyond, it is a mission that has never been more important than it is today.

I am proud that at NCSSM, we are investing in developing the next generation of scientists and researchers. I hope that as a country we remember the criticality of investing in scientific research, which is the bedrock of innovation, opportunity, and solving our greatest challenges, the ones we know of and the ones we’ve yet to discover. Amazing things happen when you bring talented student researchers together with incredible faculty and mentors and you will see just this in the research showcased in this year’s edition of *Morganton Scientific*.

I would like to thank all of the students and faculty involved in producing *Morganton Scientific*, particularly faculty sponsor Ms. Jennifer Williams and senior editors Henry Chen, Clara Lappan, and Alex Qu.

Explore and Enjoy!

Dr. Todd Roberts \

# Morganton Scientific Editors’ Note

```{raw:typst}
#v(3em)
#set par(justify: true)
```

Welcome to the third edition of NCSSM-Morganton’s premier scientific research journal: the *Morganton Scientific*! It is with profound delight that we showcase student research spanning the fields of biology, chemistry, physics, mathematics, engineering, and computer science—and even those encompassing interdisciplinary topics.

As the third editorial team behind this journal, we inadvertently assumed the noble role of serving as the third data point (n = 3)—a minimum sample size sufficient for applying statistics in scientific research—of this “experiment.” Fundamentally, then, we sought to increase the precision of our “findings” by determining what worked for previous editions of the journal and what, in turn, did not.

We recycled the selection process used to assemble our board of content and publication editors, but we inaugurated Journal Club, an ambitious but low-stakes forum for peers to deepen their understanding of scientific literature. We continued using MyST Markdown to format our articles for publication, but we initiated the first in-person, hands-on workshops throughout the revision process with editors and authors alike.

In ways unbounded by a mere set of data, this third edition proudly represents the very principles of a vibrant scientific community—one that seeks to heighten childlike curiosity while preserving the integrity of inclusive communication. Although the sample size (n = 3) will only imminently grow, it is with capacious heart that we continue laying the groundwork for what constitutes the *Morganton Scientific*.

Finally, we would like to extend our sincerest gratitude to Mrs. Jennifer Williams and Mr. Taylor Gibson for their invaluable guidance and contributions to this journal. It would have been infeasible for this third edition to come to fruition without their love in every step along the way.
